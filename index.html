Key Risks of Enterprise AI Adoption

AI accesses sensitive business data

Risks: data leaks, unauthorized access, and loss of traceability

AI acts on users' behalf in business workflows

Risks: prompt injection, unintended actions, and inadequate audit trails

AI agents use tools to execute tasks

Risks: excessive permissions, uncontrolled tool calls, and limited rollback
